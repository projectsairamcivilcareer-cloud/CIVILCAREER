📐Engineering focus

Civil Related

Internships focused on core Civil Engineering disciplines and the technical work involved in planning, design, construction and infrastructure projects.

What you may learn
  • Read basic structural and building drawings
  • Understand site measurements and quantity estimation
  • Learn concrete, steel and construction materials
  • Observe roads, buildings and water projects
Suitable for
  • Diploma and B.Tech Civil students
  • Students interested in design or site engineering
Skills you can develop
  • Drawing reading and basic estimation
  • Technical observation and reporting
🏭Workplace exposure

Industrial Exposure

Understand how engineering organizations, construction sites, laboratories and infrastructure facilities coordinate people, equipment, materials and quality.

What you may learn
  • How a construction site works day to day
  • Concrete batching, mixing and material handling
  • Quality checks for concrete and other materials
  • Site safety, teamwork and work coordination
Where you may get exposure
  • Construction companies and project sites
  • RMC plants and material testing laboratories
Skills you can develop
  • Site observation and quality-check basics
  • Safety awareness and communication
🦺Hands-on learning

Practical Learning

Develop practical skills by observing demonstrations, working with tools under supervision and relating field measurements to engineering principles.

What you may learn
  • Basic levelling and total-station survey work
  • Slump test, concrete cube test and material checks
  • Prepare simple quantity sheets and BOQ entries
  • Use AutoCAD, Revit or BIM tools at beginner level
Typical practical activities
  • Observe tests and record readings
  • Take measurements under staff supervision
Skills you can develop
  • Using basic instruments and tools
  • Recording data accurately and preparing reports
