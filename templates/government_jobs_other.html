Non-Core Government Job Categories – Central & State

These categories cover government jobs outside Civil Engineering. A Civil diploma/degree holder can apply only when the official notice accepts their qualification. Technical Civil vacancies belong on the Core Civil page.

UPSC – Central General Services

Civil Services Examination (IAS/IPS/IFS and other services), CAPF Assistant Commandant and other UPSC recruitments whose notices accept the candidate's degree. Check service-specific age, physical and qualification rules.

State PSC – APPSC / TGPSC / Other States

APPSC Group-I, Group-II and other notified general posts; TGPSC Group-I/II/III/IV and equivalent state services; plus other State PSC and state recruitment-board posts. State domicile and local-cadre rules may apply.

SSC – Central Non-Technical

SSC CGL, CHSL, MTS, CPO, GD Constable, Stenographer and Selection Post recruitments. Qualifications range from 10th/12th to graduate level depending on the post; not every exam accepts every qualification.

Railway Recruitment Boards (RRB)

RRB NTPC, Group D/Level-1, Assistant Loco Pilot, Technician, Railway Protection Force and other non-Civil technical/non-technical recruitment where eligible. RRB JE Civil and Civil engineering posts are listed under Core Civil.

Central Ministries & Departments

Non-Civil assistant, clerk, data-entry, inspector, accounts, audit, administrative and ministry-specific posts released by central departments and recruiting agencies. Eligibility varies by post.

State Government – General Recruitment

State secretariat, department assistant, clerical, revenue, municipal administration, village/field administration and other non-engineering posts advertised through state boards/commissions.

Banking & Financial Institutions

IBPS/SBI bank PO and clerical exams, RBI and other financial-institution recruitments, insurance officers/assistants and public-sector financial roles, subject to each notice.

Defence, Police & Uniformed Services

CAPF, state police, defence civilian, officer-entry and other uniformed-service recruitment. Physical/medical standards, age and education criteria are post-specific.

Education, Research & Public Institutions

General administration, non-engineering faculty/teaching, library, laboratory, project and research support roles in government institutions where the stated qualification is met.

Month-wise monitoring guide (indicative, not guaranteed)

January – MarchMonitor UPSC and State PSC calendars, SSC notifications, RRB notices, banking and central/state department recruitment portals.
April – JuneCheck SSC/RRB calendars, state Group and departmental recruitment boards, banking exams and central ministry vacancies.
July – SeptemberMonitor SSC CGL/CHSL/MTS/CPO/GD, RRB NTPC/Level-1/other notices, State PSC/board updates, banking and insurance notices.
October – DecemberCheck revised UPSC/SSC/RRB and state calendars, additional vacancies, exam dates, results and next-cycle announcements.

Month windows are indicative only and are not confirmed opening dates. Always rely on the official recruitment calendar/notification.
