DIPLOMA & B.TECH

🏗️ Civil Engineering Subjects & Topics

Use these study areas as the common Civil Engineering roadmap. Select a course below to jump to its study path.

Diploma Civil B.Tech Civil

📐 Engineering Mathematics

Mathematics used throughout Civil Engineering.

  • Linear algebra
  • Calculus
  • Differential equations
  • Probability & statistics

⚙️ Engineering Mechanics

Fundamentals of forces, equilibrium and motion.

  • Force systems & equilibrium
  • Free-body diagrams
  • Friction
  • Centroid & centre of gravity

💪 Strength of Materials

Behaviour of structural members under loading.

  • Stress & strain
  • SFD & BMD
  • Bending & shear
  • Torsion & columns

🏢 Structural Analysis

Analysis of beams, trusses, frames and structures.

  • Determinate structures
  • Deflection methods
  • Influence lines
  • Indeterminate analysis

🧱 Concrete Structures

Design and detailing of reinforced concrete members.

  • Limit state design
  • Beams & slabs
  • Columns
  • Footings & development length

🔩 Steel Structures

Design and behaviour of steel members and connections.

  • Tension & compression
  • Beams
  • Connections
  • Columns & bases

🌍 Geotechnical Engineering

Soil mechanics and foundation engineering.

  • Soil classification
  • Permeability & seepage
  • Consolidation & shear strength
  • Foundations & earth pressure

💧 Fluid Mechanics & Hydraulics

Fluid properties, pipe flow and open-channel flow.

  • Fluid statics
  • Continuity, momentum & energy
  • Pipe flow
  • Open channels & hydraulic jump

🌊 Hydrology & Irrigation

Water resources, rainfall-runoff and irrigation systems.

  • Hydrologic cycle
  • Rainfall & runoff
  • Unit hydrograph
  • Dams, canals & irrigation

🚧 Transportation Engineering

Highways, pavements, traffic, railways and airports.

  • Geometric design
  • Pavement materials
  • Traffic engineering
  • Railway & airport basics

🌱 Environmental Engineering

Water supply, wastewater, air pollution and solid waste.

  • Water treatment
  • Sewerage & wastewater
  • Air pollution
  • Solid waste management

📏 Surveying & Geomatics

Measurement, mapping and modern surveying technologies.

  • Levelling & traversing
  • Total station
  • GNSS
  • Photogrammetry & mapping

🏗️ Construction Materials

Materials used in Civil Engineering and their properties.

  • Cement
  • Concrete
  • Steel
  • Admixtures

📊 Construction Management

Planning, estimation, scheduling and project management.

  • Estimation & costing
  • Rate analysis
  • CPM & PERT
  • Contracts & equipment

🎓 Diploma Civil Engineering

Study path covering engineering mathematics, mechanics, strength of materials, construction materials, surveying, building construction, concrete, geotechnical engineering, hydraulics, irrigation, transportation, environmental engineering and estimation/management.

🎓 B.Tech Civil Engineering

Undergraduate study path covering mathematics, mechanics, structural engineering, geotechnical engineering, water resources, environmental engineering, transportation, geomatics, construction materials and construction management.
