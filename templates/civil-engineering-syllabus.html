AP SBTET Diploma in Civil Engineering – C-23 Curriculum

The following subject structure is summarized from the AP SBTET Curriculum-2023 (C-23) for Diploma in Civil Engineering. The first year follows a year-wise pattern; the remaining academic semesters follow the semester pattern. Students should confirm the applicable scheme and any later amendments with SBTET AP.

First Year

  • Theory: C-101 English; C-102 Engineering Mathematics-I; C-103 Engineering Physics; C-104 Engineering Chemistry and Environmental Studies; C-105 Engineering Mechanics; C-106 Surveying-I.
  • Practical: C-107 Engineering Drawing; C-108 Surveying-I Practice and Plotting; C-109 Physics Laboratory; C-110 Chemistry Laboratory; C-111 Computer Fundamentals Practice.

Third Semester

  • Theory: C-301 Engineering Mathematics-II; C-302 Mechanics of Solids and Theory of Structures; C-303 Hydraulics; C-304 Surveying-II; C-305 Construction Materials.
  • Practical: C-306 Civil Engineering Drawing-I; C-307 CAD Practice-I; C-308 Surveying-II Practice and Plotting; C-309 Material Testing Practice; C-310 Hydraulics Practice.

Fourth Semester

  • Theory: C-401 Construction Technology and Valuation; C-402 Design and Detailing of R.C. Structures; C-403 Construction Practice; C-404 Transportation Engineering; C-405 Irrigation Engineering.
  • Practical: C-406 Civil Engineering Drawing-II; C-407 Concrete and Soil Testing Practice; C-408 Communication Skills; C-409 Surveying-III Practice; C-410 CAD Practice-II.

Fifth Semester

  • Theory: C-501 Steel Structures; C-502 Environmental Engineering; C-503 Quantity Surveying; C-504 Advanced Civil Engineering Technologies; C-505 Construction Management and Entrepreneurship.
  • Practical / Project: C-506 Structural Engineering Drawing; C-507 Field Practices; C-508 Life Skills; C-509 Computer Applications in Civil Engineering; C-510 Project Work.

Sixth Semester

Six months of Industrial Training is prescribed in the C-23 scheme. The curriculum describes industry assessments, an institutional final assessment, a training report, demonstration of a prescribed skill and viva voce.

Important: This is a subject-structure overview, not a substitute for the official detailed unit-wise syllabus, scheme of examination or amendments. Check the official curriculum document before planning examinations.

Source: AP SBTET Diploma in Civil Engineering – C-23 Curriculum PDF.

Core Civil Engineering Syllabus Areas

Depending on the course, regulation and examination, Civil Engineering preparation commonly covers subjects such as engineering mathematics, surveying, engineering mechanics, strength of materials, structural engineering, fluid mechanics, hydraulics, geotechnical engineering, transportation engineering, environmental engineering and construction-related topics.

Diploma Civil Engineering Preparation

Diploma students can organize preparation semester by semester and study theory together with drawing, CAD, surveying and laboratory-oriented concepts. Use the applicable board and regulation syllabus as the final reference for subject names, units and examination requirements.

B.Tech Civil Engineering Preparation

B.Tech students can use the syllabus to organize university subjects, strengthen core technical concepts and connect academic study with competitive-examination practice. The applicable university regulation and course structure should be checked before following a study plan.

GATE & Government Exam Preparation

Competitive preparation can be organized by subject and topic, followed by numerical problems, MCQs, previous year questions and mock tests. Examination-specific syllabus, pattern, eligibility and notification details should always be checked from the relevant authority.

How to Use a Civil Engineering Syllabus

  1. Identify your course, university or examination and applicable regulation.
  2. List the subjects and topics that apply to your current semester or examination.
  3. Study concepts first, then practice numerical problems and objective questions.
  4. Use previous year questions and mock tests to check topic-wise preparation.
  5. Recheck the latest official syllabus or notification before an examination or application.

Civil Engineering Syllabus FAQs

Does Civil Engineering syllabus change by course or regulation?

Yes. Subject names, units, course structure and examination requirements can differ between diploma boards, universities and competitive examinations. Use the applicable current official document as the final reference.

Can the same syllabus be used for GATE and government engineering exams?

No single syllabus should be assumed to apply to every examination. GATE, SSC JE, RRB JE and state-level examinations can have different subject coverage and patterns.

What should Civil Engineering students study first?

Start with the subjects and topics prescribed for your current course or examination, then build understanding through concepts, worked problems, MCQs and revision.

Important: Examination authorities and universities can revise syllabi, eligibility rules and examination patterns. Always verify current official notifications and syllabus documents before applying or preparing for an examination.
