Diploma Civil Engineering

Build practical and technical foundations for further study, site work and competitive examinations.

Important curriculum note: Diploma Civil Engineering semester subjects differ by State Board / Directorate of Technical Education, regulation year and institute. The list below is a common study framework, not a universal semester-wise syllabus.

📚 Common Diploma Civil Engineering Subjects

01
📐

Engineering Mathematics

Mathematics used in engineering analysis and problem solving.
02
🧱

Building Materials & Construction

Cement, concrete, masonry, timber, steel and construction methods.
03
📏

Surveying / Geomatics

Levelling, traversing, total station and field measurements.
04
⚙️

Strength of Materials

Stress, strain, bending, shear, torsion and structural behaviour.
05
💧

Fluid Mechanics & Hydraulics

Fluid properties, pressure, pipe flow and open-channel flow.
06
🚧

Transportation Engineering

Highways, traffic, pavements and basic transportation systems.
07
🌱

Environmental Engineering

Water supply, wastewater, air pollution and solid waste.
08
🏢

Structural Engineering

Structural analysis, concrete and steel concepts.
09
🌍

Geotechnical Engineering

Soil mechanics, foundations, bearing capacity and settlement.
10
📊

Estimation & Construction Management

Estimation, costing, planning, scheduling and site management.
